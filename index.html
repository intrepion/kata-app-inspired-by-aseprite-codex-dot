WELCOME TO PIXEL LOOM

Small canvas. Big ideas.

Choose a tool and color, then draw. Each layer has its own cel in every frame. Duplicate a frame, change a few pixels, and press Play.

  • B / E / F / I / L / R · Pencil, eraser, fill, picker, line, box
  • Ctrl / ⌘ Z · Undo; add Shift to redo
  • Space · Play or pause the flipbook
  • Ctrl / ⌘ S · Download your editable project

Autosave is a convenience, not a backup. Save project downloads layers, frames, and colors. Export PNG downloads the active frame; the sprite sheet includes all frames.

This is an independent pixel-art kata inspired by Aseprite workflows, not Aseprite. No .aseprite support, selection tools, GIF encoding, tilemaps, or variable frame timing. Flipbooks are limited to 32 frames and 24 fps.
